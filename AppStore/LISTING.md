### Description (4000)

KanjiKanaTrainer teaches you to write Chinese characters and Japanese kana by hand, one stroke at a time. Watch a character draw itself in the correct order, hear how it sounds, then write it yourself with Apple Pencil or your finger and see how you did.

It was made for a young learner in a Mandarin immersion class, so it is simple to start, gentle with mistakes and encouraging when you get it right. It works just as well for grown-ups who want to learn proper stroke order.

WATCH IT DRAWN
• Every character draws itself stroke by stroke, in the correct order and direction
• Play through a whole set in a row, such as the numbers 1 to 10 or a kana row
• Each character is read aloud: a Mandarin voice for Chinese, a Japanese voice for kana

WRITE IT YOURSELF
• Write on the screen with Apple Pencil or a finger
• Get a score that checks what a teacher checks: the right number of strokes, in the right order and direction, in roughly the right shape
• A short hint tells you what to try next, whether to watch the stroke order again or match the shape more closely

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Every attempt is saved on your device
• Each character shows your best score and how many times you have practiced it

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Chinese numbers 0 to 30, compound numbers like 二十一, and large numbers 十 百 千 万 億
• 100 basic simplified Chinese characters with pinyin: nature, body, size and direction, objects and animals, verbs, pronouns and common words
• Theme sets for greetings, introductions, family, time, school and food
• Hiragana and katakana, row by row or all 46 at once

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your practice history stays on your device

Stroke data comes from the open KanjiVG and Make Me a Hanzi projects.
